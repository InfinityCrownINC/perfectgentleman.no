About Perfect Gentleman

Men's grooming in the heart of Sandnes.

Perfect Gentleman Barber Shop & Frisør is located at Langgata 45 and focuses on quality, comfort and consistent service for men of all ages.

The team offers classic men's haircuts, modern styles, fades, beard trims and traditional shaving in a relaxed barbershop setting.

The salon welcomes both Norwegian and international customers, with a direct and friendly approach to every appointment.
